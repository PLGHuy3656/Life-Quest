## 2. Gate I Asset Immutability & Runtime Smooth Shell Strategy
Vertex-slice inspection confirmed that the blocky staircase ("pixel") edges on `simple_jacket.glb`, `sports_jersey.glb`, `ninja_gi.glb`, and `tet_ao_dai.glb` were caused by baked quad-face deletions in the original Gate I GLBs. To eliminate all pixelated edges **without modifying any immutable Gate I `.glb` file** (`32/32` SHA256 checksums intact), `FoxCosmeticManager` builds smooth, normal-inflated runtime `SkinnedMesh` garment shells directly from Fox's own `FOX_Body_1`, `FOX_Body_2`, and `FOX_Arm_L_1/R_1` geometry, bound to Fox's exact `skeleton` & `bindMatrix` with smooth per-fragment rest-pose hems, cuffs, open-front jacket lapels, jersey stripes, ninja obi belt, and Tết Áo Dài gold trim.
