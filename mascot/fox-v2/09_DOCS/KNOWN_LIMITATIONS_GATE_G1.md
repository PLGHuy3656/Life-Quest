# Known limitations — Gate G1

Gate F limitations (`KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_GATE_F.md`) still apply, except #2 for the events G1 now covers (QUEST_COMPLETED, QUEST_EXPIRED, LEVEL_UP, STREAK_MILESTONE, STREAK_LOST, plus the new QUEST_STARTED, XP_GAINED, ACHIEVEMENT_UNLOCKED, STREAK_UPDATED). HABIT_CHECKED_IN, CHALLENGE_WON / LOST, QUEST_DEADLINE_URGENT and USER_IDLE keep their F fallbacks until G2 / G3.

| # | Limitation | Impact | Planned / mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **glTF has no shear.** NECK / ARM / TAIL_01 ignore HIPS scale in Blender (`inherit_scale NONE`); the exporter compensates with a local scale, which is exact only when the child is not rotated relative to HIPS. | Leaf bones (ear tips, hand / hat / glasses sockets, tail tip) can sit up to 5.8 mm (G1) away from the Blender pose during squash. The approved E2 GLB shows the same effect (3.8 mm). Not visible at companion sizes; it would matter for a cosmetic that must touch the body exactly during a squash frame. | Squash was reduced on the two clips that reached 13.5 mm. For Gate H, test hats / glasses on the squash-heavy frames, or bake HIPS squash into the body via a dedicated non-hierarchical squash bone in a future rig revision (needs approval). |
| 2 | **Effects are verified in headless Chrome (SwiftShader) only** for this gate. | Visual look and correctness verified in the captures; no GPU frame-time numbers for the effects (confetti ≈ 70 particles, the flame shader, the char overlay doubling the skinned fur draw calls for ≈ 3 s). | Re-measure on a GPU (one companion) before shipping; the effects can be turned off per category. |
| 3 | **The char overlay doubles the fur draw calls while visible** (one overlay mesh per fur mesh, sharing geometry and skeleton). | ≈ 3 s per `streak_lost`, then disposed. | Acceptable for a rare event. A shader-uniform approach would need a material change, which G1 forbids. |
| 4 | **The streak flame is beside the head (right side), billboarded on the Y axis.** | From the back in the full orbit view it is partly hidden by the head. | Companion views are front / 3/4; tune the anchor when a back view matters. |
| 5 | **Text effects are canvas sprites in Vietnamese ("LÊN CẤP!", "+XP").** | Not localised; fixed font from the system stack. | Move the strings to the app's i18n when the frontend integrates the companion. |
| 6 | **`level_up` grows the fox at the apex cue.** If LEVEL_UP is published while a higher-priority reaction is playing, the event is queued and the scale change waits with it (up to the queue TTL of 8 s); if it expires, the level is applied without the clip. | The app's level is always reflected; the celebration may be skipped in a burst. | By design (priority rules of Gate F). |
| 7 | **Video previews come from headless Chrome at a fixed 1/24 s step** (deterministic capture) on a cream background, not from a live GPU session. | Timing in the videos is exact; shading matches the runtime but anti-aliasing is SwiftShader's. | – |
| 8 | **The tests sample poses at 6 frames per clip** for the Blender comparison (54 samples). | A larger deviation between samples is possible but bounded by the same mechanism (#1). | The Blender QA (`anim_check_g1.py`) checks every frame for the deformation metrics. |
