# Known limitations — Gate G1.1 (cinematic animation & VFX polish)

Gate F and Gate G1 limitations (`KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_GATE_F.md`, `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS_GATE_G1.md`) still apply unless a row below supersedes them. G1 #4 ("the streak flame is beside the head") is **superseded**: in G1.1 the flame is held on the left paw.

| # | Limitation | Impact | Planned / mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The flame rides the left paw in every clip, including the E2 core clips** (`head_pet`, `tail_touch`, `wave`, walk / run). Those clips were authored before G1.1 and keep the left paw low, so during them the flame sits on the lowered paw at hip height. This is by design: the brief requires the flame to follow the paw bone throughout animations. | While petting or walking with an active streak, the flame is lower than in the `streak_active` hold pose. It never detaches (`flame_attachment_g1_1.json`). | If the product wants the flame raised during petting too, author "streak" variants of the core interaction clips (a later gate, needs approval). |
| 2 | **The chibi arms are short.** Overhead and pointing poses read at shoulder height (same as the approved E2 `wave`). The `streak_cheer` choreography therefore presents the flame **forward to the user** instead of raising it overhead: an overhead flame overlapped the head in 3/4 views. | The torch-over-the-head idea is not possible without changing the rig proportions. | – |
| 3 | **Upright flame.** The flame base is placed with a bone-local palm offset (`FLAME_SOCKET.offset` in ATT_HAND_L space); the flame body is kept upright (flames rise) and billboarded about the vertical axis, with a spring lean against the paw's motion. It does not tilt with the paw. | When the paw is rotated far (e.g. arms up in `quest_complete`), the flame still rises vertically from the palm point. | Intentional. Rotating the flame with the paw made it point sideways or down. |
| 4 | **Head clipping avoided by placement, not by depth tricks.** The flame is depth-tested. A flame behind the head is hidden by the head, as a real object would be. The G1.1 poses keep the flame paw clear of the face in front and 3/4 views; in the full orbit view from the back, the body can hide it. | Back views only. | – |
| 5 | **Effects are verified in headless Chrome (SwiftShader) only, unless the GPU section of the runtime report says otherwise.** | Visual correctness is checked in the captures. There are no GPU frame-time numbers for the new effects (ribbons, energy ring, storm cloud, badge shader, flame layers). | Measure on a GPU (one companion) before shipping; every effect category can be turned off. |
| 6 | **glTF has no shear** (G1 #1). G1.1 keeps the deviation of leaf bones vs Blender at ≤ 4.51 mm on the sampled frames (G1 had 5.79 mm). A first G1.1 `level_up` charge reached 20.6 mm (ear tip: squash held while the neck pitched down). The charge squash was capped at 0.075. | Not visible at companion sizes. | Same plan as G1 #1. |
| 7 | **`streak_cheer` and `streak_lost` start on the `streak_active` pose**, because they are only meant to play while a streak is active (the flame paw is up). If the app plays them without an active streak, the paw lifts during the 0.2 s fade-in. | A small arm motion at the start. | The runtime already plays them only from streak events. |
| 8 | **Char overlay** (the brief soot after the strike) still doubles the fur draw calls while it is visible. In G1.1 it is lighter (strength 0.32) and shorter (≈ 0.8 s visible, then a 0.6 s fade). | Rare event, then disposed. | G1 #3. |
| 9 | **Text sprites stay Vietnamese** ("LÊN CẤP!", "+XP") as in G1. | – | i18n at frontend integration. |
| 10 | **One Gate F test races real time against simulated time** ("pointer leaves → gentle return", module off). It reads 2.30° or 2.62° against a 2.5° limit depending on step speed; the approved G1 build fails it too once the timing shifts (`runtime_g1_1/f_regression_fallback/lookat_timing_ab.json`). | Not a runtime regression: the look-at and interaction code is byte-identical to G1. | Make the test's hover-exit wait use simulated time (later gate). |
| 11 | **Visual quality is not self-approved.** The automated suites pass. The visual verdict belongs to the reviewer, based on the previews, the comparison video and the flame test video. | – | Waiting for visual approval. |
