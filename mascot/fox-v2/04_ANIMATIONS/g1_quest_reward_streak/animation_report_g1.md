## 2. Streak flame

The flame is modular runtime geometry (`src/mascot/effects/StreakFlame.ts`): a Y-billboarded procedural shader flame beside the head, a soft glow and embers. Only colours change per tier:

| Tier | Days | Core | Mid | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| orange | 3–6 | `#FFD25E` | `#FF8A1F` | `#E8480F` |
| blue | 7–13 | `#B8E4FF` | `#3EA2FF` | `#1A4FC4` |
| purple | 14–29 | `#E2C7FF` | `#9A5CFF` | `#5224B5` |
| crimson (dark red / pink) | 30+ | `#FFBFD8` | `#F2427F` | `#8E0E33` |

Tier changes cross-fade. The flame can flicker, flare and extinguish (smoke). It is removable (`setStreak({active:false})`, `setEffectVisible('flame', false)`).

## 4. glTF fidelity (module vs Blender)

The rig has bones with `inherit_scale = NONE` (NECK, ARM_L/R, TAIL_01) under a HIPS bone that squashes non-uniformly. glTF and three.js always inherit scale and have no shear, so the exporter writes a compensating local scale; when a child is also rotated relative to HIPS the result can only be approximate, and leaf bones (ear tips, hand / hat / glasses sockets, tail tip) shift by a few millimetres during squash. This exists in the approved E2 GLB too.

| Measurement (sampled frames) | Max |
|---|---|
| three.js on the E2 Fox vs an independent numpy evaluation of the module data (**binding exactness**) | 0.0002 mm |
| G1 module data vs Blender | 5.79 mm (`streak_lost` f50, ear tip) |
| Approved E2 GLB vs Blender, on its own clips | 3.78 mm (`tail_touch` f35, hand socket) |

The first G1 version reached 13.5 mm (`quest_failed`: squash held while the neck pitches down; `quest_complete`: squash with both arms up). The squash on those two clips was reduced (comment in `anim_g1.py`); after that every clip is ≤ 5.8 mm, the same order as the approved E2.

## 5. Effects (runtime)

All effects live under `FOX_FX_ROOT` (follows the level scale), never modify the Fox materials and are disposed when they end or when the clip is interrupted. Props are temporary effect objects, separate from future cosmetics. Full description: `09_DOCS/FOX_RUNTIME_CONTRACT_V2.md` §12; runtime validation: `08_VALIDATION/gate_g1_runtime_report.md`.

| Category | Effects |
|---|---|
| flame | streak flame (4 tiers), flicker, flare, extinguish |
| lightning | jagged tube bolt + branch (no flash sprite under reduced motion) |
| char | overlay meshes sharing the Fox geometry and skeleton, patchy soot material, fade in 0.2 s, out over ~1 s |
| particles | confetti, star ring / burst, sparkle, XP orbs, smoke, sigh / cough puffs, soot shake, rain |
| props | flag (`ATT_HAND_R`), medal |
| text | "!" , "+XP", "LÊN CẤP!" |
